About Me

Aspiring Cybersecurity Professional & Public Speaker

Shimon Talukder Raj (Raj), online as rajtalukder159, is an aspiring cybersecurity professional and public speaker from Bangladesh, focused on red teaming, digital forensics and incident response, and web and network security.

I learn by doing. Through labs and hands-on projects, I've worked with Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite, Wireshark, Nessus, SQLMap, OWASP ZAP and Volatility. One of my projects is a Docker and Debian web server that I built, scanned and documented, with a full report of the misconfigurations I found and how to fix them.

My training so far includes the Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking Career Track (Ostad), Cybersecurity Career Path by Microsoft & LinkedIn Learning, CCNA 200-301 (SkillUp by Simplilearn), CCNP Enterprise: Core Networking (v9) course (Cisco Networking Academy), and Programming for Cybersecurity (LetsDefend). I'm also a General Member of NextGen Youth Society, where I join skills development and networking sessions.

I'm building my projects openly on GitHub, and I plan to publish technical write-ups as I grow. Outside of security, I enjoy public speaking, playing instruments, and reading.
